# Drawing visual review - 2026-10-18

All five A3 PDF sheets were rendered with Poppler at 100 dpi and inspected.
Top plans, both section sheets, button/PCB detail and evidence/clearance sheet
are legible with no clipped content or overlapping text. The button detail
caption was corrected to 12:1 to match the geometry. Matching SVG sheets retain
vector paths/text. Preview PNGs are saved in `preview/`.

Sections are sampled from the CAD tessellation at 0.04 mm; annotations use
nominal model dimensions. This is drawing/layout verification only, not
physical fit, capacity, strength, sealing, optical, acoustic or RF validation.
